Water testing

Buffalo Water

Buffalo Water conducts lead water testing. Contact the department to ask how to request a test, how to collect a sample, and how results are shared.

Visit Buffalo Water ↗
Housing assessment & education

LeadSAFE Erie County

LeadSAFE Erie County offers housing assessments and instruction on preventing lead poisoning. Ask about services available for your household.

Visit LeadSAFE Erie County ↗
Before you go: Program details may change. Please confirm services, eligibility, fees, and appointment requirements directly with each organization.

Not sure what to ask?

You can start simply: “I live in East Buffalo and want to learn about lead testing. What should I do first?” Ask whether the service is free, what information to bring, and how long results or follow-up may take.

Three ways to
get ready

01

Write down your question

Note whether your concern is about tap water, paint, dust, soil, or a child’s health.

02

Contact the right resource

Ask Buffalo Water about water testing or LeadSAFE Erie County about a housing assessment and prevention instruction.

03

Keep the next step handy

Save contact details and ask who to call if you need help understanding results or follow-up options.
