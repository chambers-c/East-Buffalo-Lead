Healthy homes start with information
Our class project goal: free lead testing kits.

We hope to help residents access free kits and encourage testing. While we work toward that goal, Buffalo Water and LeadSAFE Erie County are local places to ask about current testing and prevention support.

A clear path forward

Know the risk.
Find support.

We want East Buffalo residents to have clear, welcoming information and a direct path to trusted local programs.

01 — TEST

Check your water

Buffalo Water conducts lead water testing. Contact them to ask about the current process and how to request a test.

02 — LEARN

Ask about your home

LeadSAFE Erie County offers housing assessments and instruction on preventing lead poisoning. Ask about program eligibility.

03 — TAKE ACTION

Make a prevention plan

Get practical guidance, share what you learn, and explore support for safer housing improvements.

Why take a first step?

Lead exposure can affect health and learning, especially for young children. Testing and trusted guidance can help families understand possible sources and decide what to do next. Prevention can protect children, support peace of mind, and help build healthier homes for the whole neighborhood.
