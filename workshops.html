Where lead may be found, why children are more vulnerable, and what questions to ask a health provider.

How to contact Buffalo Water, ask about lead testing, and understand sample collection instructions.

Safer homes and prevention

Connect with LeadSAFE Erie County for housing assessments and prevention instruction; learn about lead-safe renovation questions.

Make space to exchange trusted information and identify what support residents want to see next.

A longer-term vision

Healthy homes
for the future.

We hope this project can help build momentum for future renovation efforts that reduce lead hazards and support safer housing in East Buffalo.

Renovation work in older homes can disturb lead-based paint. Residents should seek qualified guidance on lead-safe work practices and available programs before work begins.
